About me:

I am a 13-year-old born on February 13, 2012, in The Gambia, a country in West Africa. I moved to the United States when I was four years old. English was not my first language—my first languages are Soninke and Mandinka—but I learned quickly and adapted. When I was nine, I returned to The Gambia, then came back to the U.S. at age eleven. That was when I discovered soccer, a sport I quickly fell in love with. Since then, I’ve grown a lot, explored new interests, and continue learning every day as I work toward my goals.
